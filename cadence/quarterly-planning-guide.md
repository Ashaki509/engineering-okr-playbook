# Quarterly Planning Guide

A five-week cadence for setting OKRs, starting four weeks before the quarter opens. The goal is to publish aligned OKRs by the first week of the quarter, with dependencies already agreed.

---

## Timeline

| When | Step | Owner | Output |
|------|------|-------|--------|
| Week -4 | Leadership shares priorities and context | Organization leader | One-page priorities memo |
| Week -3 | Teams review last quarter and draft objectives | Team leads | Draft objectives with the priority each one serves |
| Week -2 | Teams draft Key Results and pull baselines | Key Result owners | Completed planning canvas |
| Week -1 | Alignment review across teams | Program or operations lead | Alignment map, dependency list |
| Week 0 | Final OKRs published, tracker set up | Team leads | Published OKRs and a live tracker |

Adjust the weeks to fit your planning calendar. Keep the order.

---

## Inputs to Gather First

- Organization priorities for the quarter and the reasoning behind them
- Last quarter's OKR scores and review notes
- Current baselines for the metrics the team is likely to target
- Known commitments: launches, contractual dates, compliance deadlines
- Capacity: planned time off, hiring, and the share reserved for operational work

---

## The Team Planning Workshop

Run this in Week -3 or Week -2. Plan for 90 minutes.

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 10 min | Review priorities and last quarter's results |
| 20 min | Draft objectives individually, then group and vote |
| 30 min | Write Key Results for the top 1 to 3 objectives using the formula |
| 15 min | Run each Key Result through the 4-question test |
| 10 min | List dependencies on other teams |
| 5 min | Assign an owner to every Key Result and every open question |

Use the [Quick Planning Canvas](../templates/quick-planning-canvas.md) for a single objective or the [Quarterly Planning Canvas](../templates/quarterly-planning-canvas.md) for the full workshop.

---

## Capacity Check

Before OKRs are final, confirm the plan fits the team.

1. Total the team's available capacity for the quarter.
2. Subtract the share reserved for operations, support, and maintenance.
3. Roughly size the initiatives behind each Key Result.
4. If the total is over capacity, cut an objective. Do not trim every objective a little.

---

## Exit Criteria

OKRs are ready to publish when all of these are true:

- [ ] Each team has 1 to 3 objectives with 2 to 3 Key Results each
- [ ] Every objective names the priority it serves
- [ ] Every Key Result has a baseline, a target, one owner, and a data source
- [ ] Cross-team dependencies have a named owner on the other team who has agreed to them
- [ ] The plan passed the capacity check
- [ ] The tracker is set up and the first weekly check-in is on the calendar

---

## Common Problems

| Problem | Response |
|---------|----------|
| Priorities arrive late | Draft from last quarter's priorities and revise when the new ones land |
| Baselines are missing | Make baselining a Key Result with a date in the first three weeks |
| A dependency owner says no | Escalate in the alignment review. Do not publish an OKR that relies on an unconfirmed dependency |
| Too many objectives | Ask which one the team would keep if it could only have one, then work down from there |
