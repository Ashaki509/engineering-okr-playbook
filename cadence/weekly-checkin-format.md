# Weekly Check-In Format

A 15-minute weekly review that keeps OKRs in use between planning and the end-of-quarter review. Numbers are shared in writing beforehand, so the meeting time goes to decisions.

---

## Before the Meeting

Each Key Result owner updates the tracker by end of day before the check-in:

- Current value
- Confidence rating
- One line on what changed since last week
- Any help needed

Owners who have nothing to raise can skip speaking. Their written update stands.

---

## Confidence Rating

Confidence answers a different question from progress. Progress says where the number is today. Confidence says whether the owner believes the target will be hit.

| Rating | Meaning | Expected response |
|--------|---------|-------------------|
| 🟢 On track | I expect to hit the target with the current plan | None needed |
| 🟡 At risk | I can hit the target only if something changes | Name what has to change and who can change it |
| 🔴 Off track | I do not expect to hit the target | Decide in the meeting: add help, change the plan, or revise the target |

A Key Result that moves from green to red without a yellow week in between is worth a conversation about how risk gets raised.

---

## Agenda (15 Minutes)

| Time | Topic |
|------|-------|
| 2 min | Scan the tracker together. Note any status changes. |
| 8 min | Discuss yellow and red Key Results only. |
| 3 min | Dependencies and blockers involving other teams. |
| 2 min | Confirm decisions, owners, and dates. |

---

## Written Update Template

Use this for async updates or as the tracker comment.

```
Key Result: [name]
Current: [value] (baseline [value], target [value])
Confidence: [On track | At risk | Off track]
Changed this week: [one line]
Need: [decision, help, or "nothing"]
```

---

## Rules

- Discuss exceptions only. Green Key Results are not read aloud.
- Every yellow or red item ends with a decision, an owner, and a date.
- A Key Result that stays yellow for three weeks gets escalated or re-planned.
- Changes to a target are allowed mid-quarter with a written reason and the leader's approval.
- If there are no yellow or red items, end the meeting early.

---

## When to Escalate

Raise an item beyond the team when:

- The blocker sits with another team and their owner has not responded within one week
- Hitting the target requires a tradeoff against another objective
- The Key Result no longer makes sense because circumstances changed

Bring the tradeoff and a recommendation, and say what you need decided.
