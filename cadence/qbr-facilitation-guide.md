# QBR Facilitation Guide

A quarterly business review closes one OKR cycle and feeds the next. This guide covers preparation, a 90-minute agenda, scoring, and the outputs the session should produce.

---

## Purpose

A good review answers three questions:

1. What did we achieve against what we set out to do?
2. What did we learn about how we plan and execute?
3. What will we do differently next quarter?

It is not a presentation of everything the team worked on.

---

## Preparation

| When | Who | Task |
|------|-----|------|
| 2 weeks before | Facilitator | Confirm date, attendees, and the pre-read deadline |
| 1 week before | Key Result owners | Enter final values and a score for each Key Result |
| 3 days before | Team leads | Send the pre-read |
| Day before | Attendees | Read the pre-read and add questions as comments |

---

## Scoring

Score each Key Result on a 0.0 to 1.0 scale.

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 0.0 to 0.3 | Little or no progress |
| 0.4 to 0.6 | Meaningful progress, target missed |
| 0.7 to 1.0 | Target hit or nearly hit |

For an improvement Key Result, the score is the distance covered divided by the distance planned: (final - baseline) / (target - baseline).

Agree in advance whether targets are committed or stretch. For stretch targets, a score near 0.7 is a good result. For committed targets, the expectation is 1.0.

---

## Pre-Read Template

One page per team.

```
Team: [name]          Quarter: [QX YYYY]

Objective 1: [text]
  KR1: [text]  Baseline [x]  Target [y]  Final [z]  Score [0.0 to 1.0]
  KR2: ...

What worked: [2 to 3 bullets]
What did not: [2 to 3 bullets]
What we learned: [1 to 2 bullets]
Proposed change for next quarter: [1 to 2 bullets]
Decision or help needed from leadership: [if any]
```

---

## Agenda (90 Minutes)

| Time | Topic | Lead |
|------|-------|------|
| 5 min | Purpose, ground rules, and how scores will be used | Facilitator |
| 10 min | Results at a glance across all teams | Facilitator |
| 40 min | Team discussions, focused on misses and surprises | Team leads |
| 15 min | Cross-team patterns: what repeated across teams | All |
| 15 min | Implications for next quarter's priorities and planning | Organization leader |
| 5 min | Decisions, owners, and dates | Facilitator |

Divide the 40 minutes by the number of teams. The pre-read replaces the presentation, so each team's time goes to questions.

---

## Questions That Move the Discussion

- What did we believe at the start of the quarter that turned out to be wrong?
- Which Key Result would we write differently, knowing what we know now?
- Where did a dependency help us or hurt us?
- What did we stop doing, and was that the right call?
- If this objective carries over, what changes in the approach?

---

## Ground Rules

- Scores describe the plan and the outcome. They are not ratings of people.
- A low score with a clear lesson is more useful than a high score on an easy target.
- Discuss the misses first.
- Capture decisions as they happen.

---

## Outputs

The review is complete when these exist:

- [ ] Final score for every Key Result, recorded in the tracker
- [ ] A short list of lessons, each with a change to make
- [ ] A decision for each objective: close, carry over, or revise
- [ ] Input for next quarter's priorities, sent to leadership
- [ ] A summary shared with the wider organization within one week
